Hegwer Law · GoHighLevel → MerusCase · working sample

Signed lead in, one clean MerusCase case out

When a lead reaches “Signed”, the bridge checks the data and finds the client if they already exist. Then it creates the case, adds each injury with its own POST, attaches the employer and records the referral source and signed date. If MerusCase saves something but the reply never arrives, the bridge reads it back instead of creating a second copy.
